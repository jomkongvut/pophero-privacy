Privacy Policy – PopHero

Last updated: 28 October 2026

PopHero ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Facebook Page PopHero TH to upload and schedule the page's own educational videos.

What the app accesses

The app uses the Facebook Graph API only to publish and schedule videos (Reels) on the PopHero TH page and to read the status of those videos. It uses only the permissions the page owner grants (pages_manage_posts, pages_read_engagement, pages_show_list).

What we collect

The app does not collect, store or share personal data of Facebook users. It stores only the page's own post records (video IDs, publish times, captions) on the owner's computer.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Deleting data

The page owner can remove the app's access at any time in Facebook Settings → Business integrations, and delete the local records. Contact: jomkongvut@gmail.com
