Privacy Policy – PopHero

Last updated: 29 October 2026

PopHero ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the PopHero channels (Facebook Page PopHero TH and the PopHero YouTube channel) to upload and schedule the channels' own educational videos.

What the app accesses

The app uses only the official APIs, with the permissions the owner grants when signing in:

  • Facebook Graph API – publish and schedule videos (Reels) on the PopHero TH page and read their status (pages_manage_posts, pages_read_engagement, pages_show_list).
  • YouTube Data API – upload and schedule videos to the owner's PopHero channel and read their status (youtube.upload, youtube.readonly).

What we collect

The app does not collect, store or share personal data of viewers or other users. It stores only the owner's own post records (video IDs, publish times, captions) and the owner's sign-in tokens, on the owner's computer.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Deleting data

The owner can remove the app's access at any time (Facebook Settings → Business integrations; Google Account → Third-party access) and delete the local records. See Data Deletion. Contact: jomkongvut@gmail.com
